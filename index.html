Synths/instruments:

surrealistic mg1

great analog synth, included in the reccomended freebie bundle

vital

genuinely the best synth ever, u can make absolutely anything ur heart desires with ts

gr-8

such a good synth, they have a lot of good analog presets

pg-8x

amazing analog synth, no presets by default and a bit weird looking but once you understand it's super good for making analog sounding synths

effects:

emergence

cool granular reverb, too complicated to explain but it is VERY good for making ambient sounds

youlean loudnesss meter

its made to let you see the perceived loudness vs the actual loudness, as they are two different things

mesa lite

its pretty much a free version of my goat maximus, though you get a bit less control (its a limiter so ur sounds dont go over a certain ammount)

tape fiasco

emulates the effect of stopping an analog tape, can make some super cool effects

t de esser

it helps remove that annoying sound when saying the letter "s" in a mic

Where to find plugins

Plugin Boutique

Lots of great free plugins, as well as really good deals on paid ones

SampleFocus

Free sounds made by users that upload, but u only get 3 download tokens per day, which can get annoying

PresetShare

Great for finding presets for vital and other synths, all made by the users that upload them

r/Drumkits

Weirdly enough reddit is great for finding sounds, js be careful downloading from it tho

Looperman

Pretty much js reddit but safer, people submit a bunch of loops/samples u can download royalty free

personal favorite brands and plugins

stock fl plugins

i will literally glaze tf out of fl till the end of time their stock plugins are ABSOLUTELY AMAZING, esspecialy maximus(a limiter, good for making sure ur stuff is LOUD), fruity reeverb 2(genuinely such a good reverb, best for like drums n stuff for some slight boost, but lacks a bit if ur using it as like a super big reverb), luxeverb(now this is amazing for big reverb, its pretty much built for it. its got a filter built into it so u have even more control over the reverb)

unplugred

she makes the most STUNNING and useful plugins ever, genuinely a lifesaver. sunburnt, pisstortion, crumbl and prisma are amazing!! they're all TECHNICALLY paid but u can download free version of all of them that in my experience dont limit u at all

bleepfx

they have a few free plugins that i absolutely love
